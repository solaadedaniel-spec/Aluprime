Questions

Before you order

Anything else? Ask on WhatsApp and the window code and size go straight into the message.

How do I measure, and which size do I need?

Measure the finished opening (after plastering) three times across and three times up, and use the smallest numbers. The window frame is a little smaller than the opening so your installer can fix and seal it. Please confirm the measurements with your installer before ordering. Our size guide shows which windows may fit, and we check again on WhatsApp.

Are the windows in stock?

Each window shows its stock status once we launch. Always confirm availability on WhatsApp before you travel to collect.

How do I pay?

We confirm the total price and how to pay on WhatsApp before you pay anything. Payment details are only sent from the official Aluprime WhatsApp number.

Do you install the windows?

Aluprime supplies ready-made windows; your own installer fits them. Send installation questions on WhatsApp and we will help. Each window comes with its fixing kit.

Can you send the windows to my site outside Lagos?

We deliver within Lagos only. You arrange onward transport from the factory or your Lagos delivery address, usually with your other building materials. Tell us how far they will travel and we will advise on packing.
