Will it fit?

Two sizes matter: the opening in the wall and the frame of the window. Measure once, check here, and we confirm on WhatsApp.

FIT

The opening must be a little bigger than the frame.

The opening size is the hole in the wall. The frame size is the outside of the window. The gap lets your installer pack, fix and seal the window.

How to measure

  1. Measure the finished openingAfter plastering if possible. If it is not plastered yet, say so and tell us how thick the plaster will be.
  2. Measure 3 widths and 3 heightsWidth at the top, middle and bottom. Height at the left, centre and right. Use the smallest of each.
  3. Check it is squareMeasure both diagonals, corner to corner. They should be within about 10 mm of each other.
  4. Confirm with your installerAsk your installer to check the measurements before you order.

Type your smallest measurements.

Guide only: a window may fit when the opening is about 10-40 mm larger than the frame in both directions. We confirm every size on WhatsApp before you pay.
